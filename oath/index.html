(A) Overview

Most habit trackers trust a checkbox, and a checkbox is easy to lie to. *Oath* asks for proof: snap a live photo, and AI decides whether you really kept your *word*.

The problem

Ticking a box costs nothing, so it means nothing. Streaks drift into wishful thinking and the habit quietly dies.

The idea

Put a tiny, honest bit of friction back in. Show the trainers, the page or the plate, and let AI check that the photo matches the promise.

The outcome

A native iPhone app, designed and built end to end by me, now live on the App Store with real subscribers.

Say it. Prove it.

(B) How it works3 steps
01

Make an oath.

Name it, give it a time of day (morning, afternoon, evening or anytime) and choose how it's checked: a live photo, or a simple tick. Repeating habits and one-off oaths sit side by side, so today is always one clear list.

02

Prove it.

When it's done, the camera opens. Live camera only: there's no gallery to pick from. The photo is compressed on-device and sent to a vision model that checks it's relevant to the oath. A picture of a picture doesn't count.

03

Watch it grow.

Every habit gets its own heatmap, filling in green day by day. Any one thing done secures the day for your streak, and milestones land at 3, 7, 14, 30, 60, 100 and 365 days.

Built for the long run

(C) FeaturesiOS 26

  • Weekly aims

    "Gym, five days a week." Rest days are part of the plan and never break a streak.

  • Build it, or quit it

    Start a habit or break one: count clean days, or log each slip honestly.

  • Nudges, never nagging

    A reminder when an oath is due, and one more only if today's still empty.

    Photos are checked, then discarded. Only the verdict is kept.

Under the hood

(D) Engineeringv2.1

  • Swift & SwiftUI

    Native iOS 26 app built on @Observable stores. It began as a React Native / Expo prototype; I rebuilt it natively for widgets, haptics and feel, porting the streak and date maths verbatim so no subtle timezone bugs crept in.

  • AI proof judge

    A Deno edge function sends the photo to a GPT-4.1 mini vision model. It's lenient by design: it checks relevance, not completion, so a treadmill proves a run. It rejects pictures of screens or prints, because proof has to be the real world.

  • Supabase

    Postgres with row-level security, Sign in with Apple, and offline-aware sync that queues edits and replays them on reconnect. Rate limits are enforced server-side in a single RPC round-trip.

  • RevenueCat

    Subscriptions with a free trial. Entitlement is written server-side by a signed webhook, so a tampered client can't fake access.

  • WidgetKit

    The app writes a snapshot into a shared App Group; interactive widgets tick items optimistically and the app replays them on launch. A seeded soak test checks the widget and app always agree.

  • Privacy first

    Camera is the only permission. Photos are discarded after checking, analytics are privacy-first (TelemetryDeck), and deleting your account erases everything.
